Biblical Truth

What Is Deliverance?

Deliverance is the biblical process of being set free from spiritual bondage, oppression, and strongholds through the authority of Jesus Christ. It brings healing, restoration, and renewed purpose — not just temporary relief, but complete freedom.

Freedom From:

  • Fear and anxiety
  • Trauma and emotional wounds
  • Addiction and destructive habits
  • Generational curses
  • Spiritual oppression
  • Unforgiveness and bitterness
  • Shame, rage, doubt, and rebellion

"And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free." — John 8:32

"For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power and of love and of a sound mind." — 2 Timothy 1:7
